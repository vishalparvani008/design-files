How we build, in four rules

01
Detection happens on site

Every model runs on a box beside the camera, so a result lands in the same second as the frame — and a dropped uplink never stops detection.

02
Every number links to its frame

Each figure we report carries the clip and timestamp that produced it, so an auditor asking where a number came from gets the footage, not a calculation.

03
We use your cameras, not ours

No rip-and-replace. We deploy against the feeds, sensors and controllers already installed, on partner stacks already certified in the field.

04
Counting never means identifying

For footfall, queueing and density, features are anonymised on the device and nothing identifying is stored. Face recognition is a separate, deliberate decision.
