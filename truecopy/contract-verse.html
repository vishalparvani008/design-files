Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can I use Contract Verse for employee agreements?
Absolutely. You can use Contract Verse for employee agreements as well as non-disclosure agreements, vendor contracts, dealer agreements, leases and any other high-volume contract type.
Do you provide real-time e-stamping?
Yes. The integrated NeSL platform enables real-time e-stamping alongside e-signing, with duty computed per state and unused stamps tracked and recovered.
For which verticals is Contract Verse suitable?
Contract Verse is vertical agnostic. It delivers the maximum value when you need to generate a large volume of contracts from a template — dealer agreements, vendor contracts, employee non-disclosure agreements and so on.
Does Contract Verse offer APIs to integrate into my CRM?
Yes. REST APIs and webhooks let you trigger contract generation, approval and signing from SAP, Oracle, Navision, Salesforce or any custom application, with the signed document returned to the calling system.
