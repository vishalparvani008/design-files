The context

Why the industry moved

Automotive runs on paperwork that crosses organisations — dealer agreements, transporter contracts, supplier purchase orders, vehicle handover documents. Each one used to mean printing, couriering and waiting, with no reliable view of where a file had stopped.

Digital signatures removed the physical step without removing the legal weight. Approvals that took a week close the same day, every signature carries an audit trail, and the evidence a regulator or court asks for is produced on demand rather than retrieved from a warehouse.

Use cases

Automotive, end to end

From the plant floor to the dealership forecourt, automotive documents cross more organisations than almost any other industry. Truecopy sits across those handovers — signing, stamping and recording every one so the paperwork keeps pace with the vehicle.

Why it works

Benefits

01

Security & compliance

PKI signatures with certificate chains and RFC-3161 timestamps, compliant with the IT Act 2000, eIDAS, UETA and ESIGN.

02

Sign via web browser

No install, no plugin. Signatories open a link, authenticate and sign on whatever device is in their hand.

03

Faster turnaround

Approvals that waited days in couriers and inboxes close the same afternoon, with the queue visible throughout.

04

Saves time and cost

Paper, printing, courier and storage removed from the process, and the man-hours spent chasing signatures with it.
