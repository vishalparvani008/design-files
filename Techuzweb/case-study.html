02Challenge

Three problems stood between the idea and a platform learners would stay on.

PROBLEM
[Thousands of academic videos had to play smoothly on low-bandwidth mobile networks.]
→
WHAT WE DID
[Adaptive streaming with a CDN and resumable playback, tuned for 3G and 4G.]
PROBLEM
[Plans, trials and institute-sponsored access needed one consistent billing model.]
→
WHAT WE DID
[A single subscription engine with roles for learners, institutes and admins.]
PROBLEM
[Learners dropped off mid-course without feedback on their progress.]
→
WHAT WE DID
[Interactive quizzes after each module and a progress dashboard that nudges learners back.]
